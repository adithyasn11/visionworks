Per-class precision / recall at confidence 0.35 (`eval_ppe_model.py --only reports/ppe6_test_deduplicated*.txt`); the number in brackets is the labelled boxes of that class.

**Reading it.** About two in five public test images are frames from the same videos as training images, so the published per-class numbers are optimistic, helmet precision most of all. The de-duplicated sets are small (25 to 34 helmets), so these numbers are rough. The held-out Construction-PPE set (valB) behind the main result has no copied pictures: its one close pair is the same gate camera with a different person.
